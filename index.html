Contradiction

The door in the hill is green in chapter 3 and red in chapter 12.

  • Chapter 3
    “the green door stood half-hidden in the bracken”
  • Chapter 12
    “painted a red that nobody remembered”

Nothing between the two scenes repaints the door or explains the change.
