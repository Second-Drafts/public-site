Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Who we are

The Department of Second Drafts ("we", "us") runs seconddrafts.com, a collection of writing tools. To ask us anything about your data, email privacy@seconddrafts.com or write to us at [Postal address].

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

What we don't do

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, and we only have your email address if you give it to us.
  • We don't show advertising or track you across other websites.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use personal data to train AI models.
  • Our tools don't send us what you write. It stays on your device, and when a tool makes a share link, the link carries your text after the # in the address, which browsers never send to a server. The only writing we receive is what you choose to send us in a form.
  • We don't receive your phone's motion. The Magic 8 Ball reads it on the page, only to notice a shake, and only once you allow it where your device asks. The readings are never stored or sent.

Hosting

GitHub Pages serves this site. When you visit, GitHub Pages logs your IP address for security and to keep the service running, as described in its privacy statement. Our legal basis is legitimate interest: a website can't be delivered or protected without this. GitHub keeps these logs; we don't receive them.

Analytics

If you accept, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages, tools and settings people use, so we know what to improve. Our legal basis is your consent. Nothing is loaded until you accept, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

In plain terms, we record which pages you visit, which tool settings you use, roughly how long your text is (in ranges of word counts), how you reach the site, how long you use a tool, and your device type. For the Magic 8 Ball, we record whether you typed a question, but never the question itself. We never record what you write.Google Analytics also measures some things itself: how far down a page you scroll, which links to other sites you follow, when you start and submit a form (never what you type into it), and any file downloads and video plays. Google also receives what it collects automatically, such as your IP address and browser details. For the cookies involved and how long they last, see the cookie policy.

We have linked Google Analytics to Google Search Console, which shows us which Google searches lead people to this site. The link works both ways: Search Console's search statistics appear in our analytics reports, and aggregate site statistics from Google Analytics, such as how many people visit a page, are shared with Search Console. Neither product is told who any individual visitor is.

Waitlists and forms

Some pages invite you to join a waitlist or answer a few questions, for example to hear when a tool or feature is ready. Every form is optional, says what it's for, and sends nothing to us until you submit.

If you give us your email address, we keep it with a note of which form you used and when, so we only email you about what you asked about. If a form offers extra options, such as trying an early version, we only act on the ones you tick. Joining one waitlist never signs you up for another.

If you answer optional questions, we read your answers to decide what to build and how. We don't publish them or quote them anywhere without asking you first. Some questions may invite you to describe your own writing. Share only what you're comfortable sending us.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give separately for each form and each option you tick. Cloudflare stores what you send in the EU on our behalf. It sees your IP address while handling the request and uses it to block abuse, but we don't store it with your details. When we email you, the email goes through our email provider. We don't add you to any other mailing list or share your address with anyone else.

To leave a waitlist, reply to any email from us or write to privacy@seconddrafts.com. You can withdraw from one form or one option and keep the rest, or ask us to delete everything you've sent us.

  • Google Analytics: EU–US Data Privacy Framework

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw your consent. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise a right, email privacy@seconddrafts.com. Analytics data is linked only to a random ID stored in your browser, not to your name, so we usually can't find one person's data. If you want us to try, tell us the value of the _ga cookie from your browser. Turning analytics off in the cookie settings stops any further collection. For anything you sent us in a form, write from the email address you gave us, so we can find your details.

If you are in the EU or UK, our legal bases are your consent for analytics and for forms, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the site for hosting. If a browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics. We don't respond to Do Not Track, as there is no common standard for it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect personal data from loss and unauthorized access. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee the security of anything sent over the internet.

How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. The cookies we set last as long as the cookie policy says, and you can delete them at any time in your browser.

Google Analytics deletes the record of each event, such as a page view, after 2 months, and data linked to the random ID in your browser after 14 months. The totals in its reports, such as how many people visited a page, are kept longer but aren't linked to you.

We keep what you send through a form, your email address and any answers, until we've sent what you asked for, such as the email saying a tool has launched. If we decide not to build it, we'll tell you and delete it then. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when what we collect or who we share it with changes. The date at the top shows the latest change.
