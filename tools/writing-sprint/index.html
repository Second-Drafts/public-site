How to run a writing sprint

  1. Choose a length: 10, 15 or 25 minutes, or your own, up to three hours.
  2. Press Start sprint and write. The timer counts down and the word count rises as you type.
  3. Pause if you are interrupted, or hide the timer if watching it makes you tense.
  4. When time is up, you see how many words you wrote and how long the sprint ran.

Tips for a good sprint

  • Decide what you will write before you press Start, even if it is only the next scene.
  • Do not go back to fix things. Leave a note to yourself, such as [name?], and keep moving.
  • Use the same length for a week and watch your word count. A steady number is easier to build on than a big one.
  • Treat the draft as a first draft. The second draft is where you cut and reshape.

Your draft stays in your browser

Nothing you write is sent to our servers. The draft is saved in your browser so it is waiting when you come back. There is no account and no limit on how many sprints you run.

Questions

What is a writing sprint?

A writing sprint is a short, timed stretch of writing without stopping to edit. You set a timer, write until it ends, and count how many words you got down.

How long should a sprint be?

Most writers use 10, 15 or 25 minutes, so those are one click away. You can set any length from 1 to 180 minutes. Start short if the blank page is the problem, and lengthen the sprint once starting is easy.

Do words I already wrote count?

No. A sprint counts the words you add after you press Start. If you paste in an earlier draft first, only the new words count. If you cut text during the sprint, the total never drops below zero.

Can I pause the timer?

Yes. Pause stops the clock until you resume. Stop ends the sprint early and takes you back to your draft, without a result.

Is my writing private?

Yes. Your draft is saved in your browser only, so it is still there when you come back, and it is never sent to our servers. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

What can I do with my draft afterwards?

When a sprint ends you can open the draft in Draft Analysis, which shows the length of every sentence. The draft travels in the part of the address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to any server.
