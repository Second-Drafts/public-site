How we count words and characters

Characters are easy. Every letter, space, punctuation mark and even emoji count in the total.

Words on the other hand are a little harder. We count a word as any group of letters or numbers between spaces. Hyphenated words count as one. Other dashes, double hyphens, and ellipses break up words.

Speaking time is an estimated

Speaking time takes into consideration your word count along with a common, steady speaking speed of 150 words per minute. It's helpful for estimating talks, voiceovers, and scripts. Dramatic reading may take longer, and conversational reading may take less.

Setting a limit

  • At most: stay below a max count, such as 650 words for a college essay or 280 characters for an X post.
  • At least: reach a minimum.
  • Exactly: hit a specific number, like how a six-word story or a 100-word drabble requires.
  • Around: hit a number with some leeway when the precise number matters less, like a 300-word query letter or a one-minute script.
  • Between: keep inside a range, like 250 to 650 words.

Each format is a shortcut to filling in the settings for you: fiction from six-word stories to short stories, query letters, synopses and loglines, the Common App essay, posts on X, Bluesky and Threads, and spoken scripts from 30 seconds to five minutes. If you go over, we'll show you by how much to help you iterate on your text.

Questions

Do hyphenated words count as one word?

Yes. Single hyphens join words, so "well-known" counts as one. Em dashes, en dashes, double hyphens (--) and ellipses separate words, so the two halves of "night—or morning" count separately.

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes. The character count includes spaces, punctuation, and line breaks. It counts what you see as one character, so an emoji or an accented letter is one, not two.

How long is 500 words when read aloud?

About 3 minutes 20 seconds. Speaking time assumes 150 words per minute, a comfortable pace for a talk or a voiceover. Choose "minutes aloud" as the unit to aim for a time instead of a number of words.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

Your text is saved in your browser only, so it is still there when you come back. It's never sent anywhere else.

How do I check a limit for an essay, post or contest?

Pick a format such as Common App personal essay, drabble or X post, or set your own limit: at most, at least, exactly, around or between, in words, characters or minutes aloud. The count shows how far you are from it, and when you are over, the words past the limit are marked in red so you can see what to cut.
