How words and characters are counted

A word is any run of letters or numbers between spaces. A single hyphen joins words, so well-known is one word. Em dashes, en dashes, double hyphens and ellipses separate them. Languages written without spaces, such as Chinese, Japanese and Thai, are split into words the way your browser's text tools split them.

Characters include spaces, punctuation and line breaks. An emoji or a letter with an accent counts as one character, however it is stored.

How speaking time is estimated

Speaking time is your word count divided by 150 words per minute, rounded to the nearest five seconds below a minute and to the nearest minute above it. It is a guide for talks, voiceovers and scripts. Slow, dramatic reading takes longer, and a quick conversational read takes less.

Setting a limit

  • At most: stay under a ceiling, such as 650 words for a college essay or 280 characters for a post on X.
  • At least: reach a minimum.
  • Exactly: land on a number, as a six-word story or a 100-word drabble does.
  • Around: come within 10% of a number, as for a 300-word query letter or a one-minute script.
  • Between: stay inside a range, such as 250 to 650 words.

Formats fill in the limit for you: fiction from six-word stories to short stories, query letters, synopses and loglines, the Common App essay, posts on X, Bluesky and Threads, and spoken scripts from 30 seconds to five minutes. When you go over, the words past the limit are marked, so you can see what to cut.

Your text stays in your browser

The counting happens on your device. Your text is saved in your browser so it is waiting when you come back, and it is never sent to our servers.

Questions

Do hyphenated words count as one word?

Yes. A single hyphen joins words, so well-known is one word. Em dashes, en dashes, double hyphens (--) and ellipses separate words, so the two halves of "night—or morning" count separately.

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes. The character count includes spaces, punctuation and line breaks, and counts what you see as one character, so an emoji or an accented letter is one, not two.

How long is 500 words when read aloud?

About 3 minutes 20 seconds. Speaking time assumes 150 words per minute, a comfortable pace for a talk or a voiceover. Choose "minutes aloud" as the unit to aim for a time instead of a number of words.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

Your text is saved in your browser only, so it is still there when you come back. It is never sent to our servers. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

How do I check a limit for an essay, post or contest?

Pick a format such as Common App personal essay, drabble or X post, or set your own limit: at most, at least, exactly, around or between, in words, characters or minutes aloud. The count shows how far you are from it, and when you are over, the words past the limit are marked in red so you can see what to cut.
